Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Replace the contact email below with yours before publishing.

What this app is

Our Week lets small groups ("circles") share photos that stay hidden from each other until everyone agrees to open the week.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a display name, and an optional profile photo.
  • Content you add: photos, captions, place names, comments, and reactions. If a photo contains location data, we read it to suggest a place name; you can edit or remove it.
  • Device: if you turn on notifications, a push subscription for that device.

Who can see it

Only members of the circle you posted in, and only after that circle's week is opened. Until then, other members see a blurred thumbnail and a count. People outside your circles see nothing. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and photo storage) and served through Vercel. Photos are compressed before upload.

Your choices

  • Delete any photo you posted, or leave a circle, at any time. Your photos in that circle are removed.
  • Block a person to hide their content from you.
  • Report a photo from its menu.
  • Turn notifications on or off per device and per event.
  • Delete your account from the Me tab. This removes your profile, your photos, and your memberships permanently.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
